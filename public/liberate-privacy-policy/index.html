Liberate - Privacy Policy

Liberate does not collect any information, and it does not have any analytics. Liberate runs locally on your device, and the only thing it fetches over the network is an icon for each site you block. Liberate does not read, use, or share your personal data. Liberate never "phones home".

Screen Time. To block apps and run your schedule, Liberate asks for Screen Time access. It does not read your Screen Time usage data, and the apps and sites you block never leave your device.

Site icons. To show an icon next to each blocked site, Liberate requests it from Google's favicon service using the site's domain name. Like any web request, it carries your device's IP address, and nothing else about you. Icons are kept on your device and refreshed about once a week.

If you email the developer for support, your email address and other information, including the app and device details Liberate adds to a feedback email, will be kept private and never be shared with anyone.
